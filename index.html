Round counts & barrel life

Every range session is logged against the barrel that fired it, so you always know how many rounds are on it and how much life is left.

Dope cards

Elevation and wind for every distance, per rifle, barrel and load. Check off what you've confirmed on target and print a card as a PDF.

Group measurement

Photograph a target, mark a known inch or millimetre, tap the holes. Get group size in MOA, MIL and inches.

Load development

Log charges, velocities and groups side by side and keep the proof of the load you settled on.

Cleaning reminders

A quiet "clean" badge when a rifle passes its round count since the last cleaning. You set the interval. No notifications.

Your units, your way

Yards or metres, MOA or MIL, set per rifle and easy to change. Mix them where it makes sense.
